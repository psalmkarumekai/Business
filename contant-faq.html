Frequently Answered Inquiries

What are your baseline diagnostic check fees?

We maintain an upfront call-out layout structure covering thorough machine electrical tracking diagnostic testing. If you approve our quoted restoration plan, the check fee counts directly towards the repair costs.

Do you offer warranties on replaced parts?

Yes, absolutely. Every physical component swap out we provide is backed by a full warranty on both parts and workmanship.

Do you repair standard mobile tech or laptops?

No, we do not. Patelectrix focuses exclusively on major home appliances like refrigerators, laundry machinery, built-in cooktops, and thermal kitchen heating units.
